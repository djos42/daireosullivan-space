Overview

This was a rocket that I worked on as part of a team during my time at Cranfield University. It was intended to launch a rocket that another CranSEDS team were working on simultaneously. Primarily however it was CranSEDS entry into Race2Space 2025.

My Role

I had the position of co-project manager alongside the brilliant Fernando. Together we organised the team meetings, divided the work and led the overall direction of the team.

Aims

As it was meant to be integrated into a flight vehicle, it was designed as a flightweight 2000N hybrid with 8 seconds of burn time. The original intention when we began the project was that we would also develop a tank and feed system.

The engine was to burn HDPE fuel with nitrous oxide as an oxidiser. We went for a fully aluminium combustion chamber, simple bates grain geometry with a graphite throat inside a phenolic carrier.

Results

Unfortunately the rocket did not perform as expected on the test stand.

The first problem came when we received the HDPE rod and realised that it was about 1mm shy of fitting inside our phenolic liner tube. So not only would we have to drill out each section to achieve our desired core diameter, we would also have to sand each section of our almost 1 metre long grain.
