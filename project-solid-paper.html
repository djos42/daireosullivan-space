Overview

As part of my MSc in Cranfield, I took the module Engine Systems in which I had to write a review paper on Solid Rocket Motors and present the findings at an internal conference.

Although I have always been keenly interested in spaceflight and propulsion I had only a basic understanding of solid rocket motors. This paper allowed me the chance to gain a much better overview of the technology.

Key Findings

Solid rocket motors have a long history, especially if we count the blackpowder rockets that originated in 13th century China. Although they don't offer the perfromance and and control of liquid rocket engines, they are unbeated in their simplicity. Their ability to be stored for long periods of time and offer reliable, instant thrust means that there will be demand for solid rockets far into the future.

  • Future demand will come from defence not spaceflight as reusable launchers become more common.
  • High demand is allowing new companies to be created and challenge existing primes.
  • Although new technologies such as additive manufacturing and composites materials are being adopted, the technology has not fundamentally changed in decades.
